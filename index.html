Scholarly Purpose & Institutional Mandate

Advancing Drug Discovery into Translation

NeuroMet Pharmacon-2027 is convened by the National Institute of Pharmaceutical Education and Research (NIPER), Raebareli, recognized as an Institute of National Importance by an Act of Parliament under the aegis of the Department of Pharmaceuticals, Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers, Government of India.

The congress brings together chemists, pharmacologists, drug discovery scientists, and clinicians to advance research on neuro-metabolic disorders. Integrating chemistry and pharmacology, this edition explores pharmacotherapeutics, neuropharmacology, drug repurposing, network pharmacology, and target validation to translate drug discovery into therapeutic advances.

Viksit Bharat @2047 & UN SDG 3 Alignment

Dedicated to accelerating indigenous pharmaceutical innovation, drug accessibility, and high-tier scientific workforce capacity in adherence to India's vision of global pharmaceutical self-reliance.

01. WORKSHOP

A workshop of Network Pharmacology

Hands-on training session covering target-disease network construction, biological pathway enrichment, compound-target interactions, and multi-target drug discovery paradigms.

02. MEMORIAL & SCIENTIFIC TALKS & PLENARY ORATIONS

Prestigious Academic Lectures

Memorial lectures, scientific talks, and plenary orations by researchers and clinicians covering pharmacotherapeutics, neuropharmacology, and translational drug discovery for neuro-metabolic disorders.

03. YOUNG SCIENTIST RECOGNITION

Oral & Poster Presentations

Competitive podium oral presentations and structured poster sessions for post-graduate scholars, doctoral researchers, early-career pharmacologists, drug discovery scientists, and clinicians presenting peer-reviewed original work.

Scientific Scope & Call for Papers

10 Thematic Scientific Tracks

Ten interconnected tracks spanning neuro-metabolic pharmacology, biotechnology, chemistry, AI-enabled drug design, repurposing, indigenous systems, and toxicology, with a shared focus on neurological disorders and translational drug discovery.

TRACK 01

Neuropharmacology & Neurotherapeutics

Neurodegeneration, neuroinflammation, synaptic dysfunction, epilepsy, and neuropsychiatric disorders; mechanistic target validation and experimental pharmacotherapeutics for CNS disease.

CNS PharmacologySubmit Abstract →
TRACK 02

Cerebrovascular & Neuro-Metabolic Pharmacology

Stroke, ischemia-reperfusion injury, neurovascular dysfunction, brain insulin resistance, obesity, and diabetes-associated cognitive impairment; the links between metabolic dysfunction and neurological disease.

CNS PharmacologySubmit Abstract →
TRACK 03

Cancer Pharmacology & Neuro-Oncology

Glioma, brain metastases, cancer metabolism, tumor-microenvironment interactions, anticancer target validation, and treatment resistance; mechanisms linking cancer biology with CNS complications.

CNS PharmacologySubmit Abstract →
TRACK 04

Biotechnology & Translational Disease Models

Biologics, RNA-based approaches, extracellular vesicles, disease biomarkers, and multi-omics; cellular, iPSC-derived, and organoid models for studying neuro-metabolic disorders and evaluating therapeutic candidates.

BiotechnologySubmit Abstract →
TRACK 05

Medicinal Chemistry & Target Validation

Pharmacophore design, structure-activity relationships, lead optimization, target engagement, and selectivity; discovery and characterization of chemical entities for neuro-metabolic disorders and cancer.

ChemistrySubmit Abstract →
TRACK 06

Synthetic Chemistry & Chemical Biology

Novel synthetic methods, catalysis, green chemistry, bioactive natural-product synthesis, and chemical probes; enabling chemistry to investigate disease mechanisms and support drug discovery.

ChemistrySubmit Abstract →
TRACK 07

In-silico & AI-Driven Drug Design

Molecular docking, molecular dynamics, QSAR, chemoinformatics, generative AI, and multi-omics integration; interpretable models and experimental validation of predictions for CNS, metabolic, and cancer targets.

In-silico & AI Drug DesignSubmit Abstract →
TRACK 08

Pharmacovigilance-Guided Drug Repurposing & Network Pharmacology

Safety-signal analysis, real-world data, drug-target-disease networks, systems pharmacology, and pathway analysis to generate repurposing hypotheses for neuro-metabolic disorders and cancer. Signals require validation and do not establish causality or treatment efficacy.

Drug Repurposing & Network PharmacologySubmit Abstract →
TRACK 09

Indian Indigenous Systems & Ethnopharmacology

Ayurveda, Siddha, Unani, and ethnobotanical knowledge; phytochemical characterization, formulation standardization, herb-drug interactions, safety, and mechanistic evaluation in neuro-metabolic disease models.

Indigenous Systems & EthnopharmacologySubmit Abstract →
TRACK 10

Toxicology & Safety Pharmacology

Neurotoxicity, metabolic and organ toxicity, environmental exposures, genotoxicity, and preclinical safety assessment; mechanism-based biomarkers and alternative experimental models for evaluating therapeutic risks.

Toxicology & Safety PharmacologySubmit Abstract →

Heritage & Cultural Immersion

Places to Visit in Lucknow

Experience the world-renowned architectural majesty, royal gardens, and legendary culinary heritage of the Awadh capital during your conference stay.

14 km from NIPER · Built 1784
Bara Imambara & Bhool Bhulaiya

Commissioned by Nawab Asaf-ud-Daula as a grand famine relief project. Houses the world's largest unsupported vaulted arch hall constructed without iron beams, the intricate three-dimensional maze of Bhool Bhulaiya, and the historic Baoli stepwell.

🕒 06:00 AM – 05:00 PM View on Map →
14 km from NIPER · Turkish Gate
Rumi Darwaza

The 60-foot-tall ornamental gateway modeled after the historic Sublime Porte in Istanbul. Serving as the ceremonial entrance to the Old City of Lucknow, its graceful chhatris and Awadhi floral stucco work form the iconic emblem of the city.

🕒 Open 24 Hours · Illuminated at Night View on Map →
15 km from NIPER · Palace of Lights
Chota Imambara (Hussainabad)

Constructed in 1838 by Nawab Muhammad Ali Shah. Known as the "Palace of Lights" for its breathtaking collection of antique Belgian glass chandeliers, gilded minarets, calligraphy murals, and the adjacent unfinished Satkhanda watchtower.

🕒 06:00 AM – 05:00 PM View on Map →
16 km from NIPER · 1857 Memorial
The British Residency

Preserved in its picturesque ruined state, this tranquil complex witnessed the pivotal Siege of Lucknow during 1857. Features sprawling lawns, century-old banyan trees, the historic Banquet Hall, cannonball scars, and a well-curated 1857 Memorial Museum.

🕒 07:00 AM – 06:00 PM View on Map →
13 km from NIPER · Heritage & Food
Hazratganj & Awadhi Gastronomy

The heart of Lucknow's evening stroll culture ("Ganjing"). Victorian facade architecture with premium Awadhi dining—famous for melt-in-mouth Galawati kebabs at Tunday Kababi, Dastarkhwan biryani, Sharma ki Chai, and authentic Lucknow Chikan embroidery boutiques.

🕒 Open till 11:00 PM · Culinary Heartland View on Map →
12 km from NIPER · Waterfront Park
Gomti Riverfront Promenade

A serene waterfront boulevard along the Gomti River. Features musical fountains, landscaped gardens, illuminated walking trails, and sweeping skyline views of Lucknow's modern and historic bridges. An ideal evening retreat for conference delegates.

🕒 06:00 AM – 10:00 PM View on Map →
