Scholarly Purpose & Institutional Mandate

Advancing Pharmacology into Clinical Translation

NeuroMedPharmacon 2027 is convened by the National Institute of Pharmaceutical Education and Research (NIPER), Raebareli, recognized as an Institute of National Importance by an Act of Parliament under the aegis of the Department of Pharmaceuticals, Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers, Government of India.

The congress serves as an authoritative international bridge uniting molecular pharmacologists, neuroscientists, computational chemists, and clinical investigators. Under the guidance of NIPER-Raebareli’s Laboratory of Molecular NeuroTherapeutics, this edition focuses on mechanistic drug discovery, targeted drug delivery, neurodegenerative pathophysiology, and translational toxicology.

Viksit Bharat @2047 & UN SDG 3 Alignment

Dedicated to accelerating indigenous pharmaceutical innovation, drug accessibility, and high-tier scientific workforce capacity in adherence to India's vision of global pharmaceutical self-reliance.

Competitive podium oral presentations and structured poster sessions for post-graduate scholars, doctoral researchers, and early-career pharmacologists presenting peer-reviewed original work.

Heritage & Cultural Immersion

Places to Visit in Lucknow

Experience the world-renowned architectural majesty, royal gardens, and legendary culinary heritage of the Awadh capital during your conference stay.

14 km from NIPER · Built 1784
Bara Imambara & Bhool Bhulaiya

Commissioned by Nawab Asaf-ud-Daula as a grand famine relief project. Houses the world's largest unsupported vaulted arch hall constructed without iron beams, the intricate three-dimensional maze of Bhool Bhulaiya, and the historic Baoli stepwell.

🕒 06:00 AM – 05:00 PM View on Map →
14 km from NIPER · Turkish Gate
Rumi Darwaza

The 60-foot-tall ornamental gateway modeled after the historic Sublime Porte in Istanbul. Serving as the ceremonial entrance to the Old City of Lucknow, its graceful chhatris and Awadhi floral stucco work form the iconic emblem of the city.

🕒 Open 24 Hours · Illuminated at Night View on Map →
15 km from NIPER · Palace of Lights
Chota Imambara (Hussainabad)

Constructed in 1838 by Nawab Muhammad Ali Shah. Known as the "Palace of Lights" for its breathtaking collection of antique Belgian glass chandeliers, gilded minarets, calligraphy murals, and the adjacent unfinished Satkhanda watchtower.

🕒 06:00 AM – 05:00 PM View on Map →
16 km from NIPER · 1857 Memorial
The British Residency

Preserved in its picturesque ruined state, this tranquil complex witnessed the pivotal Siege of Lucknow during 1857. Features sprawling lawns, century-old banyan trees, the historic Banquet Hall, cannonball scars, and a well-curated 1857 Memorial Museum.

🕒 07:00 AM – 06:00 PM View on Map →
13 km from NIPER · Heritage & Food
Hazratganj & Awadhi Gastronomy

The heart of Lucknow's evening stroll culture ("Ganjing"). Victorian facade architecture with premium Awadhi dining—famous for melt-in-mouth Galawati kebabs at Tunday Kababi, Dastarkhwan biryani, Sharma ki Chai, and authentic Lucknow Chikan embroidery boutiques.

🕒 Open till 11:00 PM · Culinary Heartland View on Map →
12 km from NIPER · Waterfront Park
Gomti Riverfront Promenade

A serene waterfront boulevard along the Gomti River. Features musical fountains, landscaped gardens, illuminated walking trails, and sweeping skyline views of Lucknow's modern and historic bridges. An ideal evening retreat for conference delegates.

🕒 06:00 AM – 10:00 PM View on Map →
