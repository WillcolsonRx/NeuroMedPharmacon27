10 SCIENTIFIC THEMES (CALL FOR ABSTRACTS):
================================================================================
Theme I:    Molecular Mechanisms of Drug Action & Receptor Pharmacology
Theme II:   Neuropharmacology & CNS Drug Discovery (Neurodegeneration, Stroke, Epilepsy)
Theme III:  Cardiovascular & Metabolic Pharmacology (Hypertension, Diabetes, NASH)
Theme IV:   Cancer Pharmacology & Immunotherapeutics (Targeted Biologics, Immuno-oncology)
Theme V:    Pharmacokinetics, PK/PD & Model-Informed Drug Development (MIDD)
Theme VI:   Toxicology, Predictive Organ Safety & Regulatory Toxicology
Theme VII:  Natural Products, Phytomedicine & Ethnopharmacology
Theme VIII: Clinical Pharmacology, Precision Therapeutics & Pharmacovigilance
Theme IX:   AI/ML in Drug Discovery, Chemoinformatics & Drug Repurposing
Theme X:    Regulatory Science, CDSCO/FDA Pathways & Translational Medicine
