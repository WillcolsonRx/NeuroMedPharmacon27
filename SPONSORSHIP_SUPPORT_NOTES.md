# Sponsorship and philanthropic support

Updated 26 October 2026. Commercial sponsorship base rates: Platinum INR 100,000,
Gold INR 50,000, Silver INR 25,000. Existing additional 18% sponsorship GST remains.
Delegate registration pricing is separate and GST inclusive.

Philanthropic support replaces Bronze for new inquiries. Its amount is agreed
with the Secretariat; zero stored on an inquiry denotes an unpriced contribution,
not a free or settled payment. The API rejects payment submissions for this option
until the institution agrees a contribution through its own process. No donation
amount or payment settlement is inferred from the request body.

Includes one lunch and one dinner for one supporter, no commercial deliverables,
no booth or promotional commitments, and no delegate registration. The institution
must confirm contribution handling and any applicable taxes before collecting it.
No tax exemption, deduction, or charitable receipt is advertised. Existing Bronze
records remain unchanged.

The user requested comparable web research. The ACM conference-planning page
https://www.acm.org/conferences/conference-planning could not be retrieved: the
network proxy returned HTTP 403. No external practice or tax conclusion was verified.
The design therefore uses explicit prices/inclusions and voluntary inquiry wording,
without representing a researched market strategy or obscuring the available choices.
